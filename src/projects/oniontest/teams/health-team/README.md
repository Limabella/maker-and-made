# OnionTest Health Team

`health-team` is the first project team in Maker-and-Made. The folder owns the
characters as they are integrated into OnionTest; the global character archive
continues to preserve their setting and historical source material.

## Roster

| Member | Role in OnionTest | Status | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| ONN-C | Five Flavor Onion character and player-facing interaction engine | active | [`onn-c/`](onn-c/) |
| MND-N | bounded mental-health support, safety, and psychology knowledge agent | active | [`mnd-n/`](mnd-n/) |
| TRN-N | movement, exercise, and physical-state support | joining | [`trn-n/`](trn-n/) |
| NTR-N | nutrition and daily health support | planned | [`ntr-n/`](ntr-n/) |
| CUR-N | historical research perspective represented by Marie Curie | planned | [`cur-n/`](cur-n/) |

## Boundary

OnionTest currently explores mental-health support and a psychological
counseling simulation. It is not a clinical diagnosis or treatment system.
The team boundary is intentionally broader than the current MVP so physical
training, nutrition, and historical research can join without moving the
project back to a flat entity catalog.

Project research and decision records live in
[`docs/research`](../../../../../docs/research/).
